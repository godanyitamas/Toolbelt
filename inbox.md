- [ ] Concept candidate: explicit progress state / unit of work. Correctness comes from making each unit of work, its identity and its completion explicit, recorded state, instead of relying on timing or on outputs appearing. Cases: progress tables + completion barrier (productionizing), per-date completion ledger (repartitioning), orphan retry state (late-arriving data), batch identity (`query_id`, `batch_id`) instead of a hand-written name + date `txnAppId` (observability). Ready for a concept note (repo review 2026-10-25).
- [ ] Concept candidate: physical layout follows the readers. Layout and access pattern form a contract: the layout is designed for the dominant readers (you can't please everyone equally), and every other reader, including new processes and ad hoc queries, has to take the layout into account to get the performance. Cases: business partition columns for the largest tables (productionizing); a functional column added next to `ingestion_date` (repartitioning); a downstream Gold job that did not benefit from that table's good partitions until it pruned explicitly (append-only CDC, reading the repartitioned table); liquid clustering instead of identity partitions (observability). Clarified in repo review 2026-10-25.
